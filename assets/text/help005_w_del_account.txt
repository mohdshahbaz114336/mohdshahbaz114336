ques=[[
What happens when an account is deleted?
]]

tag=[[
basic, top
]]

body=[[
In MyMoney, when you delete an account, all the associated records are also deleted. Depending on the type of the record:
<br><br>
<b>Income/Expense:</b> All the associated income and expense records will be deleted.
<br><br>
<b>Transfer:</b> Transfer records will not be deleted. Rather they will be converted to a special type of expense or income record indicating that those amounts were sent to or received from a deleted account.
