Can I use MyMoney in multiple devices?
]]

tag=[[
other, _faq
]]

body=[[
MyMoney is an offline app. MyMoney data (ie. added records, categories, etc.) remains on the specific device you use and there is no sign-in option available.
<br>
<br>
Hence, cross-device data synchronization is not possible. You should install and use MyMoney on your primary device only.
