ques=[[
What does a category mean in MyMoney?
]]

tag=[[
basic, top
]]

body=[[
A category is a label that can be applied to any type of expense or income. It specifies that particular expense or income activity. There are two types of categories: income and expense.

@img{{ w_categories }}

MyMoney allows you to create as many categories as you like (both free and pro versions). You can select your preferred icon and name for each category.
<br><br>
<b>IMPORTANT:</b> Deleting a category will also delete all the records associated with this category.

@ref{{ w_del_record }}
]]
