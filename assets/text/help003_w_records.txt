What is a record in MyMoney?
]]

tag=[[
basic, top
]]

body=[[
In MyMoney, a record is an individual entry for an expense or income activity.

@img{{ w_records }}

Records are of 3 types:
<br><br>
- An income record associates an income-category with the account into which the money is settled. Say, getting your salary (category) into your bank account (account).
<br><br>
- An expense record takes an expense-category along with the account from which the amount was spent. For example, you bought a coffee (category) and paid with cash from your wallet (account).
<br><br>
- Transfer records represent account-to-account balance transfers, which will not affect your total balance. For example, you transferred $200 from your "Card" to your "Wallet" or just withdrew $100 from ATM and put it into your wallet.
<br><br>
A record has other helpful attributes like time, date and additional notes.
<br><br>
<b>IMPORTANT:</b> Note that when you delete a record, balance changes made by that record will be reversed as if the record was never added.

]]
