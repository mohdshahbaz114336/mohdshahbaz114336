name=[[ w_del_record ]]

ques=[[
What happens when a record is deleted?
]]

tag=[[
basic, top
]]

body=[[
In MyMoney, when you delete a record it affects the balance of the associated account. The effect is the reverse of adding the record. For example:
<br><br>
- If it is an expense record the amount is added back to the account.
<br><br>
- If it is an income record the same amount is subtracted from the account.
<br><br>
- If it is a transfer record, the amount was transferred back to respective accounts.
<br><br>
Suppose, after creating a $50 expense-record from your "Wallet" account, balance is $450. Now, if you delete this record, "Wallet" balance will become $500, as it was before adding the record.
]]
