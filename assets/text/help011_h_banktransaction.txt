How to track bank transactions with MyMoney?
]]

tag=[[
howto, top
]]

body=[[
Almost always, the banks charge some extra amount for every transaction or service. To properly manage these records, it is recommended to keep a separate expense-category for "Bank charges".
<br><br>
For example, you withdrawn $250 from local ATM, and put the cash into your wallet. The bank charged you $2, but that can be considered as an expense.
<br><br>
So you should add two separate records in MyMoney: one transfer record, and one expense record.

@img{{ h_banktransaction }}

Same is the case when the bank gives you interest, in which case you should keep a separate income-category "Bank interests".
]]
