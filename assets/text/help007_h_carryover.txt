ques=[[
How to configure "Balance carry over" feature in MyMoney?
]]

tag=[[
howto, top
]]

body=[[
In MyMoney, the carry over feature does not affect the account balance. It simply calculates the total ending balance of the selected month/week/day as of now.
<br><br>
To enable/disable this feature, go to <b>Display options</b> and tap ON/OFF near "Carry over" feature.

@img{{ h_carryover1 }}

@img{{ h_carryover2 }}

When the carry-over feature is OFF, the top section shows the total balance as (Income minus expense) of the selected month/week/day.
]]
