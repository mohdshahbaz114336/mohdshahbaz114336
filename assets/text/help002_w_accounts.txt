ques=[[
What does an account represent in MyMoney?
]]

tag=[[
basic, top
]]

body=[[
In MyMoney, an account represents a source or container that can logically store money. It can be a credit card or a wallet. In some cases, the balance can become negative logically.
<br><br>
Every account has an icon and a name.
<br><br>
Suppose, if you have $500 in your wallet, then you can create a MyMoney account with a $500 initial balance and name it "Wallet".
<br><br>
The initial field is only there to match your actual wallet balance during account creation.

@img{{ w_accounts }}

There is no limit on the number of accounts you can create. For each account, you can choose an icon and a name.
<br><br>
<b>IMPORTANT:</b> When you delete an account, all the records with this account will be deleted as well. However, transfer records associating other accounts will not be deleted.
