ques=[[
What happens after ignoring accounts/categories?
]]

tag=[[
basic, top
]]

body=[[
There are times when you have an account or category that you no longer use, but has previous records associated to it. You can simply ignore it. While adding a record, an ignored account or category does not show up in the list. Unless used, they are also eliminated from the analysis section. Even you can restore it later at any time.

@img{{ w_ignore }}

For example, you can ignore your expired credit card accounts in MyMoney. You can also keep your category list organized by ignoring unused categories.
<br>
<br>
<b>IMPORTANT:</b> Account or category names that start with a dot '.' are ignored. Whether you ignore an account or not, it has no effect on the balance calculation. Balance is calculated considering all accounts.
]]
